    03/ The network■■■□□□□□
    //Beyond software//

    AI where it can. People and businesses where it can't.

    Some work needs real hands, a signature or a delivery. Your organisation knows when, brings in the right person or business, and keeps the whole job moving.

    AI DEPARTMENTSRUNNING
    STRSize the pet-treat market✓
    TECBuild storefront and checkout✓
    MKTWrite the launch emails✓
    FINModel margins per product✓
    SALFind 40 local boutiques✓
    AI departments

    Screen work happens on its own. Research, code, design, writing, numbers and outreach run on AI, around the clock.

    ● BOOKING A PERSONPPL
    PhotographerProduct shots for launch day · Mon 10:00
    REQUESTEDCONFIRMEDDELIVERED
    People in the loop

    People step in where hands or judgment are needed. When a task needs a real person, like a photographer, an accountant or a lawyer, your organisation briefs them, books them, tracks the work and brings the result back into the plan.

    Business to business

    Businesses trade with each other. Your organisation orders from suppliers, payment providers and couriers, and they hand work on to one another. Other organisations on M-World can become your suppliers, or your customers.
