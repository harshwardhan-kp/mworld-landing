          How does M-World run my business?
          M-World reads your prompt and any files you add, then sets up Strategy, Management, Technology, Finance, Marketing and Sales. Management breaks the plan into sprints and hands each department its tasks. You follow every task, booking, order and credit on your board.
          
      
          What happens when a task needs a real person?
          Your business recognises work that needs hands or judgment, like a product shoot, a contract review or a tax check. It writes the brief, books a suitable person, tracks the job and pulls the result back into the plan. Every booking shows up on your board.
          
      
          How do businesses on the network work together?
          Your business can order from suppliers, payment providers, couriers and other services, and those businesses can hand work to each other, for example a supplier passing an order to a packaging partner and then to a courier. Other businesses on M-World can become your suppliers or your customers.
          
      
          What is the difference between Semi- and Fully-Autonomous?
          On Semi-Autonomous you decide when each department works: marketing today, sales tomorrow. On Fully-Autonomous your business runs continuously in sprints, planning and executing across every department without waiting for your instruction.
          
      
          What are credits?
          Credits pay for the work your departments do. Semi-Autonomous includes 200 credits per billing cycle and Fully-Autonomous includes 600.
          
      
          Do I need a credit card to start?
          No. The Starter plan is free and needs no card. A sentence describing your business is enough to create your business, explore every department and build your first app.
